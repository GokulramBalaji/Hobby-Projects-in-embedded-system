CRITICAL SAFETY RULE: NEVER POWER THE 25 KG·CM SERVO FROM ESP32!
Under rapid clutch pull strokes, the 25 kg·cm high-torque servo draws between 1.8A and 2.8A stall current. Powering it directly from the ESP32 USB 5V or 3.3V pins will cause catastrophic voltage brownouts, resetting the ESP32 continuously and potentially destroying the onboard voltage regulator.

Operational Guide

3. How to Connect with Your Mobile Phone (Direct Wi-Fi)

The ESP32 features a built-in emergency SoftAP (Access Point) fallback server. You can control the transmission directly from your phone browser without needing a home Wi-Fi router or PC running!

1
Power On the ESP32 Controller
Connect the ESP32 to a 5V USB charger or power supply. The onboard blue LED will light up. If it cannot find a preconfigured router within 6 seconds, it automatically launches its own standalone Wi-Fi Access Point.
2
Open Wi-Fi Settings on your Phone
On your iPhone or Android device, go to Settings → Wi-Fi. Scan for available networks and tap on:
AMT-CONTROLLER-AP
3
Enter Wi-Fi Password
When prompted for the network password, enter:
amtadminpassword
(Note: Your phone might display "Connected without internet" — this is completely normal because you are directly connected to the microcontroller).
4
Open Browser & Turn on the Mobile Dashboard
Open Chrome, Safari, or Firefox on your phone, and type into the address bar:
http://192.168.4.1
5
Control Transmission & Monitor Live RPM
The mobile-optimized dashboard will load immediately! You will see:
  • Real-Time RPM: Shows live engine flywheel RPM via WebSocket.
  • Gear Display: Large numeral indicator showing active gear (N, 1, 2, 3, 4).
  • Cockpit Buttons: Tap UPSHIFT (▲), DOWNSHIFT (▼), or NEUTRAL to shift instantly.
  • Auto / Manual Toggle: Switch between automatic RPM threshold shifting and manual control.
  • Emergency Stop Button: Large red button to immediately kill engine ignition and open clutch.

4. How to Connect with Your Laptop & Turn on the Full Cockpit

There are two ways to operate from a laptop: (A) Quick Direct Browser, or (B) Full Industrial Cockpit Application.

Method A: Quick Direct Access (Standalone ESP32 Server)

1. Connect your laptop Wi-Fi to AMT-CONTROLLER-AP (Password: amtadminpassword) OR ensure both ESP32 and laptop are connected to the same home Wi-Fi network.

2. Open your browser (Chrome or Edge) and navigate to:
• If on AP: http://192.168.4.1
• If on Home Wi-Fi: http://amt.local (or the ESP32's assigned router IP address).

Method B: Full Industrial Cockpit Web Application (Next.js + MQTT + SQLite)

1
Run the 1-Click Startup Script
In Windows File Explorer, open your project folder E:\CH DOWNLOAD\AMT\AMT_CONTROL_SYSTEM and double-click:
START_AMT.bat
2
Automated Subsystem Boot
The script automatically launches:
• MQTT Broker: Port 1883 (Mosquitto or embedded Aedes broker).
• Node.js Backend: Port 5000 (Express REST API, SQLite WAL database, WebSockets).
• Next.js Frontend: Port 3000 (Automotive instrument cluster dashboard).
3
Automatic Browser Launch
The startup script automatically launches your default browser to:
http://localhost:3000
4
Explore the 12 Specialized Cockpit Views
From the top navigation bar, you can seamlessly navigate through:
1. Dashboard (Radial tachometer, gear numeral, 10-phase sequence visualizer)
2. RPM Monitor (Waveform charts, PPR settings, glitch filter)
3. Transmission (Paddle shift controls, shift timing audit log)
4. Servo Control (Clutch 15° engage / 95° disengage calibration, slider)
5. Stepper Control (Jog -100/-10/+10/+100, homing, gear step presets)
6. Auto Mode (Gear upshift/downshift RPM thresholds, hysteresis, speed mapping)
7. Telemetry (High-speed multi-variable stream with latency counter)
8. Analytics (Performance score 0-100, shift duration distribution)
9. Diagnostics (Subsystem self-tests, sensor disconnect detection)
10. Data Mgmt (1-click CSV export of shift history, RPM telemetry, fault logs)
11. Configuration (Hardware pin layout, Wi-Fi credentials, MQTT host editor)
12. Emergency (Large E-Stop kill switch, failsafe latch, remote ESP32 reboot)

5. Safe Bench Testing & Calibration Procedure

Before installing the actuators onto a real engine or vehicle transmission, perform this mandatory bench test checklist:

1
Hardware Simulation Mode Verification (Zero-Hardware Test)
You can test the entire dashboard and shift state machine on your laptop even before plugging in the ESP32! In the top navigation bar of http://localhost:3000, click the "SIMULATOR: OFF" button to switch it to "SIMULATOR: ON". You will observe simulated RPM revving, automatic upshifting from Neutral to 4th gear, clutch disengagement animations, and shift log generation!
2
Bench Calibrating the Clutch Servo
With the 25 kg·cm servo mounted to your clutch linkage:
• Open Tab 4: Servo Control.
• Verify the resting engaged angle is set to 15° (zero preload on release arm).
• Click "DISENGAGE CLUTCH (95°)" and check that the clutch plate completely separates without the servo horn physically binding against mechanical stops.
• Adjust angles using the slider and click "Save Calibration to ESP32 Flash".
3
Calibrating Stepper Motor Shift Gate Coordinates
With the NEMA 17 coupled to the gear shift selector drum/fork:
• Open Tab 5: Stepper Control.
• Click "SET ZERO (NEUTRAL)" when the gearbox is physically in Neutral.
• Use the Jog buttons (+10, +100) to move the mechanism until 1st Gear clicks in cleanly. Record the step count (e.g. 500 steps).
• Repeat for Gear 2 (1000 steps), Gear 3 (1500 steps), and Gear 4 (2000 steps).
• Click "Save Step Presets to ESP32 Flash".
4
Testing Emergency Stop Failsafe
While a shift is occurring, hit the physical red mushroom button or click EMERGENCY STOP in the software cockpit:
• The engine cut relay must immediately energize (cutting spark/fuel).
• The stepper motor pulses must immediately stop.
• The clutch servo must snap to the open disengaged safety angle (95°).
• The status indicator LED will strobe rapidly.

6. Quick Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Reference

Observed Symptom Probable Root Cause Prescribed Engineering Remedy
ESP32 brownouts / reboots during shift 25 kg·cm servo drawing > 2A from ESP32 pins Isolate servo supply. Connect PCA9685 V+ to independent 6V 5A-10A power supply with common ground.
V183 IR reads 0 RPM or erratic spikes Comparator sensitivity trimpot or ambient light Adjust blue trimpot on V183 until LED toggles on flag pass. In Tab 2, increase Glitch Filter to 300 µs.
Stepper motor buzzes but does not turn Coil A and Coil B wire pairing inverted on L298N Swap Coil leads: ensure Black & Green go to OUT1/2 (Coil A), and Red & Blue go to OUT3/4 (Coil B).
L298N driver heats up excessively NEMA 17 low resistance (~2.4Ω) drawing high current Firmware automatically de-energizes coils after 400ms idle. For heavy duty, replace L298N with TMC2209.
Web Dashboard shows "OFFLINE" Wi-Fi station failed to connect to router Connect phone or laptop to fallback hotspot AMT-CONTROLLER-AP and browse to http://192.168.4.1.
E-Stop Latched permanently NC button switch contact open or wire disconnected Ensure mushroom button is twisted clockwise to release. Check continuity on GPIO 35.
SYSTEM SHUTDOWN: To safely stop all background servers, close data streams, and preserve SQLite database records cleanly, double-click STOP_AMT.bat in the project root directory.
